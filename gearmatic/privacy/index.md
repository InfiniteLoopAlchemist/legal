# GearMatic Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy explains what the GearMatic Bot Discord app, operated by Infinite Loop Alchemist, stores about you and why.

## What we store

- Your Discord user ID, and the ID of the server a command was used in, so the app can remember your exports and settings.
- The character exports you paste: game version, class, spec, level, equipped items with gems and enchants, and the score computed from them. An export contains no account name, no BattleTag and no login details.
- Which users and servers hold an active subscription, as reported by Discord. We do not receive payment details.
- A log of app events (command used, when, and by which user ID) to diagnose problems.

We do not store messages other than the exports you paste into the app's own commands, and we do not read your Blizzard account.

## Why we store it

To run the features you ask for: scoring your gear, keeping your last export so you do not have to paste it again, comparing members of a guild, and fixing bugs. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who else sees it

- Discord, because every command and reply passes through Discord.
- Members of a server can see the scores of other members who chose to paste an export in that server. Nothing is shared across servers.

## How long we keep it

Your score stays until you replace it with a newer paste, and is deleted after 180 days without one. Event logs are deleted after 90 days. When a server removes the app, that server's guild data is deleted on request.

## Your choices

- Delete your stored export with the app's delete command, or ask in the Addon Alchemist Discord server to have everything about your user ID removed, and we will do it.

## Where the data lives

On a server we control in the United States. Standard access controls apply, and only the operator has access.

## Children

The app follows Discord's minimum age. We do not knowingly store data about anyone below it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do.
